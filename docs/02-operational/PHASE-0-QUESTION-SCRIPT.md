# CORTE IBÉRICO — Phase 0 Question Script

## Business

1. What is the concrete Christmas 2026 commercial objective?
2. Which products/SKUs are priorities?
3. What is the available stock/capacity?
4. What budget is available for Meta?
5. Under what conditions can budget scale?
6. What historical period should be treated as the baseline?

## Measurement

1. Where is a real sale recorded?
2. Which system is the economic source of truth?
3. How are refunds and cancellations represented?
4. Which events are expected from Pixel?
5. Which events are expected from Conversions API?
6. Who owns the implementation?

## Meta

1. Which Business Portfolio owns the assets?
2. Which Ad Account is in scope?
3. Who currently has access?
4. What campaigns are active?
5. What objectives and optimization events are being used?
6. Which claims about Andromeda, audiences, virality or scaling are based on account evidence?
7. Which are only hypotheses or external interpretations?

## Scope

1. What exactly is expected from the audit?
2. What is expected from execution?
3. Who can approve technical changes?
4. Who can approve media changes?
5. Who owns creative/content?
6. What is the expected reporting cadence?
7. What is explicitly outside scope?

## Close

Ask for one owner and one deadline for every unresolved evidence item.
